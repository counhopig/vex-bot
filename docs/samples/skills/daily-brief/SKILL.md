# Daily brief

1. Read today's note `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and yesterday's, if they exist.
2. Read `MEMORY.md` for deadlines and recurring commitments.
3. Reply with at most five bullet points: what is due, what was left open, and one suggestion for the first task.

Keep the tone defined in `SOUL.md`. Do not invent tasks that are not in the notes.
